How does AI Mood Detection work?

Our app uses advanced AI algorithms to analyze your mood through multiple methods:

  • Camera: Facial expression analysis
  • Voice: Tone and speech pattern detection
  • Text: Writing style and word choice analysis
  • Questionnaire: Structured mood assessment

Is my data secure?

We prioritize your privacy and data security:

  • End-to-end encryption
  • Secure cloud backup
  • No third-party data sharing
  • Complete user control over data

How accurate is the mood detection?

Our AI uses machine learning to continuously improve accuracy. By combining multiple detection methods, we provide a comprehensive mood analysis. However, mood detection is complex, and results may vary.
